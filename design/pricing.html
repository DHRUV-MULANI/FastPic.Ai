Everything you need to know about FastPic.ai pricing and billing.

How is storage calculated on FastPic.ai?
FastPic.ai uses a 3-tier photo processing system for every image you upload:
  • Thumbnail — ~50 KB, used for instant grid browsing
  • Preview — ~500 KB, used for gallery viewing
  • Master — up to 25 MB, your lossless original for final delivery

Storage is counted against the master file size only. The thumbnail and preview tiers add only ~2.2% overhead — so a 10 MB RAW file counts as roughly 10.22 MB against your quota, not 30 MB.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?
Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your dashboard under Settings → Billing.
  • Upgrades take effect immediately, and you're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing cycle.
  • Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Your current features remain active until then.

No contracts, no cancellation fees — your photos are always yours.

What happens if I exceed my storage limit?

We'll never delete your photos or lock you out unexpectedly. Here's what happens:

  • At 80% usage, you'll receive an email notification.
  • At 95% usage, a banner alert appears in your dashboard.
  • At 100% usage, new photo uploads are paused — but all existing galleries remain fully accessible to you and your clients.

You can upgrade your plan instantly to resume uploads, or archive older events to free space.

Do you offer a free trial? What's included?

Yes — every plan includes a 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

During the trial you get:

  • Full access to all features of the plan you choose
  • 10 GB of storage to test uploads and AI recognition
  • Up to 3 live events with guest gallery access
  • AI face recognition on up to 1,000 photos

At the end of your trial, you can subscribe or export all your data — no lock-in.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods available in India and internationally:

UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) Debit & Credit Cards Net Banking Wire Transfer (Enterprise)

All transactions are secured by Razorpay and PCI-DSS compliant. GST invoices are issued for all Indian business subscriptions.
