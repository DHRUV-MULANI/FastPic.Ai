FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How does AI face recognition work without internet access?
FastPic.ai runs its RetinaFace detection and ArcFace embedding models server-side on our GPU infrastructure. Your photos are processed securely in our private cloud — no third-party AI APIs, no data leaves our servers. 512-dimensional face vectors are stored alongside your photos and used for lightning-fast similarity search.
What happens to my photos if I cancel my subscription?
We give you a 30-day grace period after cancellation during which all your photos remain fully accessible and downloadable. You can export your entire library at any time. After 30 days, your data is permanently deleted from our servers following GDPR-compliant procedures. We will never hold your work hostage.
Can guests download original master files?
No — by default, guests can only view watermarked Smart Previews via the guest gallery. You control whether to enable original file downloads on a per-event basis. When enabled, each download generates a unique time-limited signed URL that expires after your configured window (15 min to 7 days), ensuring zero permanent public exposure.
What file formats are supported for upload?
We support all major photographic formats: JPEG, HEIC, PNG, WebP, AVIF, and RAW files including CR2, CR3 (Canon), NEF (Nikon), ARW (Sony), DNG (Adobe), ORF (Olympus), and RW2 (Panasonic). RAW files are stored as-is in the Master Vault tier while derivatives are generated from embedded JPEG previews.
How fast is the AI processing pipeline?
Our GPU-accelerated pipeline processes an average of 1,200 photos per minute for thumbnail and preview generation, and approximately 800 photos per minute for face detection + embedding extraction. For a typical 1,000-photo wedding shoot, you can expect full processing (all 3 tiers + AI indexing) to complete in under 2 minutes.
